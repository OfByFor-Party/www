# The Founding Charter of Of By and For the People

**Adopted by sovereign local chapters — April 2026**

### **Preamble**
We, the people, recognizing that the current political system has abandoned the working citizen in favor of special interests and top-down control, establish the OfByFor.Party. We exist to return power to the local level, promote self-reliance, and serve our communities. We operate strictly on the donation of time, sweat, and localized action. We do not buy elections; we earn them.

---

### **Article 1: Sovereignty & Bottom-Up Structure**
* **Total Autonomy:** Every local chapter operates as a fully independent, sovereign unit.
* **No National Bosses:** There is no national committee, no party chairman, and no top-down hierarchy dictating local operations. Coordination happens only by mutual consent.
* **Zip Code Organization:** Chapters organize and identify themselves using standard 5-digit zip codes (e.g., ofbyfor72921) to bypass chaotic county and district lines.

### **Article 2: The Zero-Dollar Mandate**
* **No Campaign Money:** OfByFor candidates and chapters will absolutely not accept, solicit, or spend monetary campaign contributions.
* **Sweat Equity Only:** All campaigning, organizing, and promotion must be done through donated time, word-of-mouth, and local networking. There is no party treasury and no fundraising.

### **Article 3: Serve Without Reward**
* **The True Private Sector Mandate:** Candidates must come from the working private sector (small business owners, tradespeople, employees, farmers). The political and administrative class is permanently disqualified.
* **The 20% Anti-Crony Rule:** No individual may run for office or hold chapter leadership if more than **20%** of their personal household income, or more than **20%** of the gross revenue of any business they own, is derived from government contracts, public funding, or federal/state subsidies. 
* **The Contract:** Every candidate and chapter officer must publicly sign and live by the legally enforceable "Serve Without Reward" contract. They must certify their revenue independence under this contract. 
* **No Perks:** Candidates refuse all corporate PAC money, insider trading, special pensions, or healthcare benefits not available to the general public. Violation means immediate expulsion.

### **Article 4: Protection from Infiltration**
* **Membership:** Open only to U.S. citizens in the local area who sign the Charter and Serve Without Reward pledge.
* **Vetting:** New members must attend at least three open meetings and be approved by a 2/3 vote of active members.
* **Anti-Operative Clause:** No member may hold active membership in any other political party or PAC that accepts corporate, union, or dark-money funds.

### **Article 5: The Swing-Vote Strategy**
* **Targeted Leverage:** The national electoral goal is to elect 15 to 20 Representatives to the House and 2 to 4 Senators.
* **No Permanent Alliances:** These officials will not align with Democrats or Republicans. They will withhold their "swing votes" until OfByFor priorities are written into legislation.

### **Article 6: Core Principles (Non-Negotiable)**
All chapters must adhere to these foundational tenets:
* **America First:** Prioritizing domestic strength and ensuring domestic industries thrive before looking abroad.
* **Small Government & Radical Transparency:** Stripping away bureaucratic overreach. Every government action, contract, and vote must be easily accessible to the average citizen.
* **Local Resilience:** Bolstering communities by supporting private-sector initiatives that localize food supply chains, create independent energy grids, and protect regional fuel supplies.

### **Article 7: Transparency, Accountability & Amendment**
* All meetings are open and recorded; all decisions and finances are public.
* Any member can call for an immediate recall by majority vote.
* This Charter can only be amended by unanimous consent of all active chapters. Until then, it stands as written.
