A New Declaration of Independence from the Two-Party System

When in the course of human events it becomes evident that a political system designed to serve the people has instead enslaved them to endless division, corruption, and self-interest, it is the right — and the duty — of the people to dissolve the bonds that have become destructive...

We, the frustrated, the forgotten, the double-haters, the independents, and every American who is sick of choosing the lesser evil, declare our independence from the two-party duopoly that has hijacked our Republic.

The Grievances We Can No Longer Tolerate

  • Both parties have turned government into a career for the connected, not temporary service.
  • Both parties are owned by donors and special interests while we pay the price.
  • Both parties stoke rage to stay in power and leave our communities weaker.
  • Both parties promise change but deliver the same top-down control and backroom deals.

Therefore We Declare These Self-Evident Truths

  1. Government exists to serve the people — not rule them. Power flows from the bottom up.
  2. No more career politicians. Every candidate signs the Serve Without Reward contract.
  3. No campaign contributions. Everything is donated time and local volunteer effort.
  4. Purple is our color. Red + Blue = People First.
  5. Small government, maximum transparency, America First.
  6. We are the swing vote: 15–20 House seats and 2–4 Senate seats to force real change.
  7. Every local chapter is fully sovereign and infiltration-proof.

We mutually pledge to each other our lives, our fortunes, and our sacred honor — not to any party label, but to the restoration of a government that truly belongs to the people.

Signed in the spirit of 1776 — The Citizens of Of By and For the People
