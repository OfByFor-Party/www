The End of the Political Machine

The Great Betrayal: Millions of Americans feel completely unrepresented by the current two-party duopoly and the top-down, money-driven political machine. The system has abandoned the working citizen in favor of special interests. We are ruled by a permanent political class that profits from our division while our local communities are systematically hollowed out.

The True Private Sector Guarantee

Governance belongs to the working citizen, not the administrative state. We are building a system completely insulated from government bloat and crony capitalism.

  • Working Class Only: Candidates must come from the working private sector (small business owners, tradespeople, employees, farmers). The political and administrative class is permanently disqualified.
  • The 20% Anti-Crony Rule: No individual may run for office if more than 20% of their personal household income, or more than 20% of the gross revenue of any business they own, is derived from government contracts, public funding, or federal/state subsidies.
  • The Zero-Dollar Mandate: We absolutely do not accept, solicit, or spend monetary campaign contributions of any kind. Campaigning is executed strictly through donated time, sweat equity, and localized action.
  • Serve Without Reward: Every candidate must publicly sign a legally enforceable contract refusing all corporate PAC money, insider trading, special pensions, or healthcare benefits not available to the general public.

The Strategy: The Leverage of the Swing Vote

We do not need to win the Presidency to break the system. By electing just 15 to 20 Representatives to the House and 2 to 4 Senators, our elected officials will act as a unified bloc. We will not align with the Democrats or Republicans. We will withhold our crucial "swing votes" until OfByFor policies are written into legislation.

Our Core Demands

We exist to return power to the local level, promote self-reliance, and serve our communities. Until these pillars are met, our bloc will not yield:

  • America First: We prioritize domestic strength, secure national interests, and ensure domestic industries thrive before looking abroad.
  • Radical Transparency & Small Government: We demand a return to "back to the basics" governance. Every government action, contract, and vote must be easily accessible and understandable to the average citizen.
  • Local Resilience: Communities must become anti-fragile by supporting private-sector initiatives that localize food supply chains, create independent energy grids, and protect regional fuel supplies.
